15 prompts for spiritual business owners

Each one works in Claude or ChatGPT. Fill in the gold brackets with your own details, then copy and paste.

Content

Prompt 1

Turn a client win into a post

I'm a tarot reader [or coach, or healer]. This week a client had a breakthrough: [describe what changed for them in 2 or 3 sentences, your own words, no names]. Before you write anything, ask me up to 3 questions about my voice and what made this moment matter. Then write a short social post about it that sounds like me talking.
Prompt 2

Create a week of content

I'm a tarot reader [or coach, or healer]. I want a week of posts ready before Monday. This week I'm inviting people to [your offer, freebie or event]. What's on my mind in my work right now: [2 or 3 sentences, your own words]. Before you write anything, ask me up to 3 questions about who I want to reach, how I talk, and what I want people to do after reading. Then write 7 posts, one for each day. Give each one a plain first line, the full caption in my voice, and one clear action at the end. Mix teaching posts, story posts and one post that asks people to reply, and make at least two of them invite people to [the offer above].
Prompt 3

Find your four content pillars

I'm a tarot reader [or coach, or healer]. I want a set of content pillars I can rotate through every week, so every post has a home before I sit down to write it. Before you give me anything, ask me up to 3 questions: what I sell, who I'm trying to reach, and what I've most enjoyed making lately. Then give me exactly 4 pillars. For each one, tell me what it's for, who it's speaking to, and 3 example topics I could post this month. At least one pillar has to connect to something I actually sell. Name the pillars in my language.
Prompt 4

Turn this week's readings into three post ideas

I'm a tarot reader [or coach, or healer]. My readings this week kept coming back to one theme: [describe it in 2 or 3 sentences, your own words, no client names or details]. Before you write anything, ask me up to 3 questions about who I want to reach and what I want them to do after reading. Then give me 3 content ideas built on that theme: one that teaches something, one told from my side of the table, and one that asks my audience a question. Give me a first line for each that I could post as is.
Prompt 5

Turn one testimonial into three posts

I am a tarot reader, coach, healer, or spiritual business owner. I have permission to use the client testimonial below. Keep the client's meaning and exact result intact. Remove names and private details when needed. Testimonial: [paste the testimonial] The offer connected to this result is: [name the offer and what it helps people do] Before you write, ask me up to three questions about what happened, which part of my process mattered, and what I want a new reader to understand. Then create three different social posts from the same testimonial: 1. A proof post centered on the specific result or change the client described. 2. A teaching post that explains one useful part of the process behind that result. 3. An invitation post written to the person who wants a similar result and needs a clear next step. Give each post a plain first line, one complete idea, and one primary action. Keep every claim grounded in the testimonial and my answers. Do not invent details, emotions, timelines, or results.

Offers and sales

Prompt 6

Audit your sales page

I'm a tarot reader [or coach, or healer]. Here is the full text of my sales page for [name the offer]: [paste the page]. The person I'm writing it for is [describe them in 1 or 2 sentences]. If anything about my offer or that person isn't clear from the page, ask me up to 3 questions first. Then score the page from 1 to 10 on five things: how clearly it says what changes for the buyer, how specific it is, how clearly it names who it's for, how well it names the problem they're feeling right now, and whether it sounds like me. For each score, quote the line that earned it. Then give me the three changes that would help most, in order, and rewrite the weakest section in my voice.
Prompt 7

Write the welcome email for a new subscriber

I'm a tarot reader [or coach, or healer]. Someone new just joined my email list from [name the freebie or signup form they came through]. Here is everything I offer, with links: [your freebie, anything low-cost, and your main offer]. Before you write anything, ask me up to 3 questions: what they were hoping to get when they grabbed that freebie, the one thing about me that makes the rest of my emails make sense, and how I actually talk. Then write my welcome email as one complete email: deliver the thing they came for at the top, a short hello in my voice, one line on what my emails bring each week, and a plain list of everything else I offer with the links, so nobody has to go hunting for it. Close with a P.S. that asks one small question they can answer in one line, so they know replying is welcome.
Prompt 8

Turn a repeated client question into a product

I'm a tarot reader [or coach, healer, or spiritual business owner]. My clients keep asking me this question: [paste the exact question in their words]. I usually answer by teaching them: [paste the explanation you give]. Before you create anything, ask me up to 3 questions about when this comes up, what my clients want to do next, and which part of my answer they use most. Then outline one small interactive product I could build from this teaching. Give it a clear name, one job, three sections, one useful interaction in each section, and the exact action a client can take when they finish. Keep the scope small enough for a first build.
Prompt 9

Pick which product to build first

I am a spiritual business owner with real clients. I am considering three products: 1. [product idea] 2. [product idea] 3. [product idea] Here is what my clients have asked for, postponed, or needed help with: [paste your notes in their words, with names and private details removed]. Ask me up to three questions about the client need, what I already teach, and the time I can give this build. Then compare the three ideas using four criteria: strength of client evidence, clarity of the product's one job, how quickly I can make a useful first version, and whether I have a clear way to offer it to current clients. Recommend which idea I should build first. Explain the evidence behind your recommendation, name the smallest useful version, and give me the first three build decisions to make. Do not create the product yet.
Prompt 10

Raise an old offer from the dead

I am a tarot reader, coach, healer, or spiritual business owner reviewing an older offer. The offer was: [name it and explain what the buyer received]. Here is the material I still have: [sales page, emails, posts, workshop recording, worksheets, buyer questions, testimonials, or feedback]. Here are the real numbers I can find: [sales, clicks, replies, attendance, completion, refunds, or repeat requests]. If a number is missing, write "unknown." First, separate the facts from my assumptions. Tell me which parts earned attention, which parts created confusion or hesitation, which useful result still matters to my current clients, and which evidence is missing. Then ask me up to five questions before you recommend one of three paths: revive it, reshape it, or leave it archived. If you recommend revive or reshape, give me the smallest test I can put in front of my current audience before I rebuild the full offer. Keep the test focused on one result and one clear invitation.

Brand images

Run these five in order, in one ChatGPT chat, so each image builds on the last.

Prompt 11

Your colors and fonts

Create a brand palette and font pairing for [your business name], [what you do] for [who you serve]. The brand should feel [three words for the feel]. Give me eight colors with hex codes in these roles: two primary, two secondary, one light neutral, one dark neutral and two small accents. Then give me three Google Fonts: one for headings, one for subheadings and labels, one for body text. For each color and each font, add one line on where it goes on my posts and pages. Then create one image: the eight colors as circles in different sizes, each labeled with its role, on a plain white background.
Prompt 12

Update your brand logo

Attach your current logo, and your palette image if you made one with prompt 11.

Here is my current logo for [your business name]. I want an updated version that people who already know me will still recognize. Keep [what has to stay: the symbol, the name, a color]. Use my brand colors [attach your palette or list your colors] and make it feel [three words for the feel]. Create four updated versions, one at a time, each as its own square 1:1 downloadable file, going from a light refresh to a bolder change. Isolate every logo on a plain white background. Spell [your business name] exactly as written. When I tell you which one I pick, create a version of it with no text, for my profile picture.
