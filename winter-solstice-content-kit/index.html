Prompt 1

Put the whole set in your brand colors

Do this one in ChatGPT Work. Drop in the style grid and paste:

Use the attached image as the visual reference for the subjects, composition, lighting, mood, and photographic style. Recreate the nine scenes as nine separate, full-resolution square images. Generate and deliver each image as its own downloadable file, one image at a time. Do not create a collage, contact sheet, mood board, grid, or combined image. Apply my brand colors throughout the complete set: [your brand colors]. Incorporate the colors naturally into the clothing, candles, ribbons, berries, textiles, tarot cards, crystals, jewelry, and decorative details. Keep the evergreen, the snow and the candlelight so the set still reads as winter solstice. Keep skin tones natural and retain the warm, dramatic, low-light atmosphere and lush mystical editorial feeling of the reference. Create these nine individual images: 1. A woman wearing a crown of evergreen and holly, holding a lit red candle 2. A woman in a red hooded cloak holding a glowing lantern in a snowy forest 3. Three red pillar candles with dried orange slices, holly and pinecones 4. Hands fanning three gold-edged tarot cards over evergreen boughs 5. Tarot cards on a snow-dusted table among red candles and greenery 6. Hands tying dried orange slices and berries into an evergreen wreath 7. A crystal grid among pinecones, cinnamon sticks, holly and red candles 8. A freckled woman dusted with snow, holly in her hair, beside a candle 9. A white stag standing on a snowy forest path lit by lanterns Keep the set visually cohesive. Match the reference image's subjects, close framing, photographic realism, rich texture, light direction, and intimate atmosphere. Each image must feel complete on its own and use the maximum available resolution.

You get all nine photos back as separate files, still winter solstice, with your colors worked into the candles, cloaks, cards and greenery.

Prompt 2

Make a new image in this style

Drop the style grid into ChatGPT and paste:

Use the attached image as the color palette and style. Create an image of [what you need] for my [Instagram post / sales page / freebie cover], [1:1 for a post / 3:2 for a sales page / 9:16 for a story].

Fill the first bracket with your own work: a solstice altar for the longest night, a candlelit tarot spread for the year's turning, a cup of tea beside a journal, a wreath on a reading-room door. The size bracket sets the shape, so your image fits where it is going.

Prompt 3

Build a matching set

Do this one in ChatGPT Work. Drop in any photo from the pack, or an image ChatGPT made you, and paste:

Use this image as the style. Create a set of ten images that match it for my [your kind of business]. Then give me three matching backgrounds I can put text on.

When an image misses, ask again in different words. To take something out, tell ChatGPT what to remove and circle the spot so it knows exactly where.

Prompt 4

Plan a seven-part series

Do this one in ChatGPT Work too. Drop in the style grid and paste:

Use the attached image as the color palette and style. Create a seven-image series for my [your kind of business] on [your topic], one image per post, that teaches my followers [what they will learn]. Each image carries three pieces of text: 1. A headline of 3 to 6 words that names the idea of that post. 2. One practical tip, one or two short sentences, that a follower can use today. 3. A small series badge in the same corner of every image, reading "[series name] · 1/7", then 2/7, and so on, in the same font, size and color each time. Spell every word exactly as written. Keep all text inside the image with clear space around the edges, large enough to read on a phone. Keep the palette, light and look the same across all seven. Before you create any images, show me the seven headlines and tips so I can approve or change them.

Pick a topic your people already ask you about: seven rituals for the longest night, a week of solstice tarot pulls, seven ways to rest through the dark season, a week of gratitude prompts for your clients. Approve the headlines and tips first, so every post teaches something before ChatGPT draws it. Post one a day and you have a full week that looks like it belongs together.
