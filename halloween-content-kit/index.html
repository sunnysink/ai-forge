Prompt 1

Put the whole set in your brand colors

Do this one in ChatGPT Work. Drop in the style grid and paste:

Use the attached image as the visual reference for the subjects, composition, lighting, mood, and photographic style. Recreate the nine scenes as nine separate, full-resolution square images. Generate and deliver each image as its own downloadable file, one image at a time. Do not create a collage, contact sheet, mood board, grid, or combined image. Apply my brand colors throughout the complete set: [your brand colors]. Incorporate the colors naturally into the clothing, velvet and lace, textiles, tarot cards, candles, crystals, books, jewelry, and decorative details. Keep the pumpkins and the candlelight so the set still reads as Halloween. Keep skin tones natural and retain the warm, dramatic, low-light atmosphere and lush mystical editorial feeling of the reference. Create these nine individual images: 1. Tarot cards on black velvet among pumpkins and lit candles 2. A veiled priestess in black lace and gold jewelry by candlelight 3. A black cat beside a crystal ball on a draped altar with small pumpkins 4. A hooded witch holding a glowing lantern in a pumpkin patch at dusk 5. Hands with gold rings resting on a pumpkin offering with apples 6. A tarot reader with folded hands behind a candelabra at her reading table 7. A cauldron of crystals and dried roses with smoke and candlelight 8. An open spell book with an antique key among black candles 9. A woman resting her face in her hand, surrounded by candlelight and glass Keep the set visually cohesive. Match the reference image's subjects, close framing, photographic realism, rich texture, light direction, and intimate atmosphere. Each image must feel complete on its own and use the maximum available resolution.

You get all nine photos back as separate files, still Halloween, with your colors worked into the velvet, candles, cards and crystals.

Prompt 2

Make a new image in this style

Drop the style grid into ChatGPT and paste:

Use the attached image as the color palette and style. Create an image of [what you need] for my [Instagram post / sales page / freebie cover], [1:1 for a post / 3:2 for a sales page / 9:16 for a story].

Fill the first bracket with your own work: a black cat on a tarot table, a candlelit altar for a Samhain ritual, a cauldron of crystals, a pendulum over a spell book. The size bracket sets the shape, so your image fits where it is going.

Prompt 3

Build a matching set

Do this one in ChatGPT Work. Drop in any photo from the pack, or an image ChatGPT made you, and paste:

Use this image as the style. Create a set of ten images that match it for my [your kind of business]. Then give me three matching backgrounds I can put text on.

When an image misses, ask again in different words. To take something out, tell ChatGPT what to remove and circle the spot so it knows exactly where.

Prompt 4

Plan a seven-part series

Do this one in ChatGPT Work too. Drop in the style grid and paste:

Use the attached image as the color palette and style. Create a seven-image series for my [your kind of business] on [your topic], one image per post, that teaches my followers [what they will learn]. Each image carries three pieces of text: 1. A headline of 3 to 6 words that names the idea of that post. 2. One practical tip, one or two short sentences, that a follower can use today. 3. A small series badge in the same corner of every image, reading "[series name] · 1/7", then 2/7, and so on, in the same font, size and color each time. Spell every word exactly as written. Keep all text inside the image with clear space around the edges, large enough to read on a phone. Keep the palette, light and look the same across all seven. Before you create any images, show me the seven headlines and tips so I can approve or change them.

Pick a topic your people already ask you about: seven Samhain rituals for your clients, a week of ancestor work prompts, a week of shadow work prompts, seven ways to use one tarot card. Approve the headlines and tips first, so every post teaches something before ChatGPT draws it. Post one a day and you have a full week that looks like it belongs together.
