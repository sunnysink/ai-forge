# Build prompt — my link-in-bio page

Paste this into Claude (or Claude Code) to build it. Swap the bracketed bits for
your real links and brand, or leave them and let Claude ask.

---

Build me a link-in-bio page that replaces my Linktree. It is the one page my QR code, my Instagram bio, and my emails all point to. One screen: someone lands, taps the link they want, and leaves. It has to load fast on a phone and look like mine.

On the page: my name, one line on what I do, my photo, and a button for each place I send people. Put the one I most want tapped at the top. My links: [BOOKING], [MEMBERSHIP], [FREEBIE OR EMAIL LIST], [INSTAGRAM], [ANY OFFER]. A small footer with my handle.

The look. Do not build a generic Claude artifact, and avoid every recognizable generic Claude pattern: no single centered card floating on a purple or blue gradient, no default system font, no emoji used as bullet points, no "Here is your page" heading, no rounded-corner-everything. Make the design feel custom to my brand. [My brand is the Forge: warm firelight on a dark ground, deep copper and ember and gold, Cormorant Garamond headings, Hanken Grotesk body. OR: use my Forge design system skill.] It should look like I paid a designer.

Hard rules:
- One single self-contained index.html file, named in all lowercase. Everything inline, no separate files, no frameworks, no build step.
- Mobile first. Most people open this from a phone. Big tap targets, nothing tiny.
- It has to work when I just open the file. I am not editing code afterward.
- Leave my real links as obvious placeholders like [BOOKING] so I can find and swap them.

Ask me up to 3 questions first if anything is unclear. Then build it in one go.

---

## Notes

- **It is one page.** A link-in-bio is not a website. No Home/About/Services, no
  tabs, no routing. That belongs to a full site build, which is a different thing.
- **The anti-generic clause is the part worth keeping** from the website prompt.
  It is the line that stops the page reading as "made by Claude." Everything else
  about a routed multi-page site does not carry over to a link-in-bio.
- **Placeholders in brackets** so swapping your real links is find-and-replace.
