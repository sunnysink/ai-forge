Prompt 1

Put the whole set in your brand colors

Do this one in ChatGPT Work. Drop in the style grid and paste:

Use the attached image as the visual reference for the subjects, composition, lighting, mood, and cute illustrated 3D style. Recreate the nine scenes as nine separate, full-resolution square images. Generate and deliver each image as its own downloadable file, one image at a time. Do not create a collage, contact sheet, mood board, grid, or combined image. Apply my brand colors throughout the complete set: [your brand colors]. Incorporate the colors naturally into the witch hats, capes, tablecloths, tarot cards, candles, crystals, potions, and decorative details. Keep the pumpkins, the moon and the lantern glow so the set still reads as Halloween. Keep the sweet, playful characters and the cozy, magical night-time atmosphere of the reference. Create these nine individual images: 1. A smiling skeleton in a witch hat and cape carrying a lantern down a glowing village street 2. A smiling ghost with a halo reading three tarot cards at a draped table, a black cat beside it 3. A ghost holding up a tarot card over a deck among candles and crystal balls 4. A ghost with a wand stirring a bubbling purple cauldron beside an open spell book 5. A witch hat and broom resting on a porch among pumpkins and lanterns, a ghost peeking from the doorway 6. A black cat in a witch hat with a glowing crystal ball 7. A little witch carrying a lantern and a tarot card through a puddle-lit street 8. A little witch resting her chin on her hand at a tarot table, a ghost beside her 9. A little witch on the porch steps holding a jack-o'-lantern, a skeleton friend and a ghost beside her Keep the set visually cohesive. Match the reference image's subjects, close framing, cute illustrated 3D style, glowing light, rich texture, light direction, and intimate atmosphere. Each image must feel complete on its own and use the maximum available resolution.

You get all nine scenes back as separate files, still Halloween, with your colors worked into the hats, capes, cards and candlelight.

Prompt 2

Make a new image in this style

Drop the style grid into ChatGPT and paste:

Use the attached image as the color palette and style. Create an image of [what you need] for my [Instagram post / sales page / freebie cover], [1:1 for a post / 3:2 for a sales page / 9:16 for a story].

Fill the first bracket with your own work: a ghost shuffling tarot cards, a black cat on your reading table, a skeleton holding your planner, a little witch pulling a card by moonlight. The size bracket sets the shape, so your image fits where it is going.

Prompt 3

Build a matching set

Do this one in ChatGPT Work. Drop in any photo from the pack, or an image ChatGPT made you, and paste:

Use this image as the style. Create a set of ten images that match it for my [your kind of business]. Then give me three matching backgrounds I can put text on.

When an image misses, ask again in different words. To take something out, tell ChatGPT what to remove and circle the spot so it knows exactly where.

Prompt 4

Plan and post a seven-part series

Four steps, all in one ChatGPT Work chat, so each step builds on the last.

Step 1 · Plan the series

Start a new chat with no image attached and paste:

Help me plan a seven-part series for my [Instagram / Facebook / wherever you post] for my [your kind of business] on [your topic]. It should teach my followers [what they will learn]. For each of the seven posts, give me a headline of 3 to 6 words that names the idea of that post, and one practical tip, one or two short sentences, that a follower can use today. Keep it to text for now. We'll make the images next.

Pick a topic your people already ask you about: seven tarot cards that look scarier than they are, a week of Samhain prompts, seven cozy self-care rituals for October. Then read all seven. In the same chat, change any headline or tip that doesn't sound like you, until you'd post every one.

Step 2 · Make the images

In the same chat, drop in the style grid and paste:

Use the attached image as the color palette and style. Create the seven images for this series, one at a time, each as its own square file. Put each post's headline and tip on its image, spelled exactly as we wrote them, with clear space around the edges and large enough to read on a phone. Keep the palette, light and look the same across all seven.

ChatGPT pulls different scenes out of the grid for each post, so the seven look like one set without repeating.

Here's a seven-day series I made from this kit with Prompt 4: 7 Days of Spooky Tarot, one card a day, each with a reading tip.

Step 3 · Write the captions

Same chat:

Now write a caption for each of the seven posts, for [Instagram / Facebook]. Open each one with a line that makes someone stop scrolling, teach the tip in a few short sentences, and end with [what you want them to do: comment, save, visit your link]. Keep each caption under 150 words.

Paste in a caption you've written before and ask ChatGPT to match how you write.

Step 4 · Make the story graphics

Same chat:

Now turn each of the seven images into a 9:16 story graphic, one at a time, each as its own file. Keep the same scene, palette and style, extend it to fill the tall frame, and put the headline in large text in the middle of the frame, clear of the top and bottom edges. Add [your call to action, like "Link in bio" or "Comment READ"] just above the bottom edge.

Post the feed image and its story on the same day. Seven days of posts and stories from one chat.
